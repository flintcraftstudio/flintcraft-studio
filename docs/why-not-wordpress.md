# Why this beats a WordPress template

*A prospect-facing explainer. Applies to every FlintCraft demo vertical, not
just the chiropractor — keep it plain-spoken and honest.*

Most small-practice websites are a WordPress theme with a dozen plugins bolted
on. It works, until it doesn't: it's slow, it breaks on update night, and the
parts that matter for getting found — speed, structured data, accessibility —
are an afterthought you rent from someone else. Here's what we build instead,
and why it's better for you.

### One fast binary, not plugin sprawl

Your whole site is a single compiled program. There's no stack of plugins each
adding queries, scripts, and a place for things to go wrong. Fewer moving parts
means fewer ways to break and far less to think about.

### It renders in milliseconds

Pages are assembled and sent in a few milliseconds, not after a chain of
database lookups. Visitors see your site almost instantly — and Google has said
for years that speed affects how you rank and how many people stay.

### Near-perfect Lighthouse, built in — not faked

Google's own quality scorecard (Lighthouse) grades performance, accessibility,
best practices, and SEO. We build to the top of that scale from the start
rather than patching toward it later. It's a real, checkable number, not a
promise.

### You own your SEO and structured data

The "rich results" you see in Google — your star rating, hours, and location
showing right in search — come from structured data. On a WordPress site that
usually lives inside a review/SEO plugin you don't control and can't take with
you. Here it's written into your own pages, from your own content. It's yours.

### Accessible by default

The site works with a keyboard, with a screen reader, and at the contrast levels
real people need — because that's how it's built, not a compliance plugin
sprinkled on top. That widens who can reach you and reduces legal risk.

### Nothing to patch on Tuesday night

There's no plugin-update treadmill and no surprise "white screen of death" after
an auto-update. The site you launch is the site that keeps running. Maintenance
is calm and predictable.

### Your content is structured and version-controlled

Your copy, doctors, services, and reviews live as clean, structured data — not
buried in a page builder's tangle. Every change is tracked, reversible, and easy
to reason about. If you want to update an hour or a phone number, it's a small,
safe edit, not a gamble.

---

**The honest version:** a WordPress template gets you online. This gets you a
site that's fast, findable, accessible, and durable — one you actually own,
built the way the big, well-funded practices' sites are built, at a price that
makes sense for a Montana practice.
